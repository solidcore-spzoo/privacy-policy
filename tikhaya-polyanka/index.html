Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 30.10.2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application "Quiet Glade" / «Тихая Полянка» (the "Application"), package com.mikhail912.tikhayapolyanka.

The operator is SOLIDCORE spółka z ograniczoną odpowiedzialnością (SOLIDCORE sp. z o.o.), ul. Adama Mickiewicza 37, lok. 58, 01-625 Warszawa, Poland. KRS 0000981587, NIP 5252915575, REGON 522587550.

1. Data in the Application

The Application does not create an account and does not ask for email, name, phone number, location, photos, microphone, or advertising identifiers for ads.

Only game progress and settings are stored locally on the device: coins, tasks, puzzles, pictures, coloring, language, and audio. This is required for the Application to work after it is closed. This is not a registration and is not sent to the developer's own servers.

2. Subscriptions and purchase data

The Application offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly and annual) that remove daily free limits. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, not by the developer directly.

To unlock Premium and restore purchases, the Application uses RevenueCat (privacy policy). RevenueCat may process an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase / subscription history needed to validate entitlements. The developer does not receive your card number or full payment credentials; those stay with Apple or Google.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings. Deleting the Application does not cancel an active subscription by itself.

3. Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains until you clear the Application data or uninstall the Application. The developer cannot access the device and cannot remotely restore, export, or delete your local data.

Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their policies. To request deletion of RevenueCat customer data related to the Application, contact contact@solid-core.net.

4. Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal data and do not use advertising networks or marketing trackers in the Application. Purchase validation is shared only with Apple, Google, and RevenueCat as needed to provide the subscription.

5. Permissions

The Application plays bundled music and voice audio. It does not use the microphone and does not request audio recording. It does not need contacts, photos, camera, or location.

6. Third Parties

The Application uses:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play — billing and store account data under their policies;
  • RevenueCat — subscription status and purchase history for Premium access.

App stores and the device operating system may collect technical data under their own policies. The developer does not control that. This policy page is a static site: the host may keep a technical log (for example an IP address) when the page is opened. The developer does not collect or use those logs.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is a children's game for kids and parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children such as name, photos, voice, contacts, or location. Purchases must be made by a parent or account holder through the store account. A parent can delete local progress by clearing the app data or uninstalling the Application.

8. Changes

The current version is always posted on this page. The update date is shown above.

9. Contact

Email: contact@solid-core.net. Phone: +48 511 168 410.

You may write to ask for access, correction, or deletion. The developer cannot remotely delete local data on the device. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (UODO).

Приложение не создаёт аккаунт и не запрашивает почту, имя, телефон, геолокацию, фото, микрофон или рекламные идентификаторы для рекламы.

Управлять подпиской или отменить её можно в настройках Apple ID или аккаунта Google Play. Удаление Приложения само по себе подписку не отменяет.
